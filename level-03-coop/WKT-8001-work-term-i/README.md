# WKT-8001: Cooperative Education Work Term I

* **Course Code:** WKT-8001
* **Duration:** 14 Consecutive Weeks (Full-time: 35-40 hrs/week, 420-560 Total Hours)
* **Status:** Paid Work Placement
* **Prerequisites:** Successful completion of all Level 01 courses and minimum 2.7 GPA in GEP-1001

---

## 1. Course Description
Students apply their cumulative Big Data and Business Intelligence skills in a professional public sector or corporate enterprise environment. Working under the guidance of an on-site or virtual industry supervisor, students contribute to production pipelines, data models, or executive dashboard deliverables. A designated Algonquin College Co-op Faculty Advisor conducts a mid-term consultation and evaluates the final Work Term Technical Report.

---

## 3. Work Term Technical Report Rubric

The student must author a 15–20 page technical report documenting:
1. **Organizational Profile:** Departmental mandate and business intelligence context.
2. **Technical Problem Statement:** Architectural bottleneck, data scale challenge, or reporting gap addressed.
3. **Methodology & Architecture:** Detailed data flow diagrams, security controls (PBMM/CCCS), technology stack evaluation.
4. **Implementation & Outcomes:** Code structures, optimizations, and measurable performance improvements.
5. **Personal Learning Reflection:** Competency growth across the 10 Program VLOs and EES skills.
