# Government of Canada Personnel Security Screening Playbook

* **Mandate:** Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat (TBS) Standard on Security Screening
* **Target Credentials:** Reliability Status & Secret (Level II)

---

## 1. Overview of Security Levels

| Clearance Level | Authorized Access | Standard Verification Period | Typical Processing Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Reliability Status** | Protected A & Protected B assets | Past 5 Years | 4 – 8 Weeks |
| **Secret (Level II)** | Classified (Secret / Protected C) | Past 10 Years | 3 – 6 Months |

---

## 2. Mandatory Verification Checkpoints

1. **Proof of Identity:**
   * Two pieces of government-issued ID (Passport, Canadian Driver's License, Provincial Health Card).
2. **Biometric Fingerprinting:**
   * Must be taken digitally by an accredited service provider (e.g., Commissionaires) and submitted to the RCMP Canadian Criminal Real Time Identification Services (CCRTIS).
3. **Five to Ten-Year Residential History:**
   * Complete contiguous physical street addresses with no gaps greater than 2 weeks.
4. **Employment & Education History:**
   * Verifiable supervisors and university/college registrars.
5. **Credit History Verification:**
   * Credit check via Equifax/TransUnion (identifies high debt ratios that could represent financial coercion risks).
6. **Out-of-Country Verifications (For International Applicants):**
   * Certified Police Clearance certificates from any country lived in for more than 6 continuous months over the past 5 years.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Submission Workflow

```
[Student in GEP-1001 (Sept/Oct)]
        |
        v
1. Request Departmental Security Screening Package from Hiring Manager / Co-op Coordinator
        |
        v
2. Complete Form TBS/SCT 330-23E (Personnel Screening, Consent and Authorization)
        |
        v
3. Schedule Digital Fingerprinting with Departmental Security Officer (DSO) or Commissionaires
        |
        v
4. Submit Dossier to DSO -> CSIS / RCMP / Credit Bureau background verification initiated
        |
        v
5. Clearance Certificate Granted -> Ready to commence Co-op Work Term with Protected Data
```
