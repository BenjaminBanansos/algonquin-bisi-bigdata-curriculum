# GEP-1001: Public Sector Co-op Readiness & Security Clearance Prep

* **Course Code:** GEP-1001
* **Contact Hours:** 21.0 Hours (1.5 hrs/week × 14 weeks)
* **Delivery Mode:** Professional Seminar / Career Coaching
* **Prerequisites:** Admission to Program

---

## 1. Course Description
Prepares students to secure competitive paid co-operative education work terms across Government of Canada departments, Crown corporations, and National Capital Region high-tech employers. Provides comprehensive orientation on federal recruitment vehicles (FSWEP, Post-Secondary Co-op, Non-advertised appointments), Statement of Merit Criteria (SoMC) targeted resume crafting, STAR behavioral interview techniques, and detailed guidance through the **Personnel Screening & Security Clearance process (Reliability Status and Secret Level II)**.
