# Academic Policies, Evaluation & Integrity Standards

---

## 1. Grading Scale & Evaluation Standards

Algonquin College's standard grading system for postgraduate certificates applies to all courses in the BISI program:

| Percentage Grade | Letter Grade | Grade Point Equivalent | Academic Achievement |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| 90% - 100% | A+ | 4.0 | Exceptional Achievement |
| 85% - 89% | A | 3.8 | Excellent Achievement |
| 80% - 84% | A- | 3.6 | Very Good Achievement |
| 77% - 79% | B+ | 3.3 | Good Achievement |
| 73% - 76% | B | 3.0 | Satisfactory Achievement |
| 70% - 72% | B- | 2.7 | Minimum Acceptable for Honors |
| 67% - 69% | C+ | 2.3 | Acceptable Achievement |
| 63% - 66% | C | 2.0 | Passing Standard (Minimum Program GPA) |
| 60% - 62% | C- | 1.7 | Sub-Standard |
| 50% - 59% | D- to D+ | 1.0 - 1.5 | Marginal Pass (Individual Course Only) |
| 0% - 49% | F | 0.0 | Failure |

* **Passing Grade:** A minimum grade of 50% (D-) is required to pass any individual course.
* **Co-op Eligibility:** Students must achieve a minimum cumulative GPA of **2.7 (B-)** in Level 01 to be approved for participation in the Co-op Work Term competition.

## 2. Late Submissions & Extension Protocols

1. **Scheduled Lab Work:** Lab assignments are due weekly at 23:59 on Sunday.
2. **Late Penalty:** Submissions received within 24 hours of the deadline incur a flat **10% penalty**. Submissions received between 24 and 48 hours incur a **25% penalty**. Work submitted more than 48 hours late will receive a mark of zero unless a documented medical or emergency extension was granted prior to the due date.

## 3. Academic Integrity & Artificial Intelligence (AI) Policy

In strict alignment with the **Treasury Board Secretariat Directive on Automated Decision-Making** and Algonquin College Directive AA48:

* **Acceptable AI Use:** Generative AI tools (ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Claude) may be utilized for syntactical debugging, regular expression generation, and clarifying algorithmic theory.
* **Mandatory AI Disclosure:** Any code, architecture diagram, or analysis substantially generated or assisted by an LLM must include a standard disclosure comment block specifying:
  1. Tool used and prompt text.
  2. The specific block of code influenced.
  3. Verification and testing performed by the student.
* **Prohibited AI Use:** Submitting entire lab solutions, written policy briefs, or Algorithmic Impact Assessments drafted verbatim by generative AI without manual technical implementation and critical defense constitutes academic fraud.
