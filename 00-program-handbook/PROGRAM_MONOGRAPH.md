# Program Monograph: Business Intelligence Systems Infrastructure (BISI) - Big Data & Public Sector Specialization

* **Institution:** Algonquin College of Applied Arts and Technology
* **Faculty:** School of Advanced Technology
* **Credential:** Ontario College Graduate Certificate (Postgraduate)
* **Program Code:** 1514X03FWO (Gov / Big Data Stream)
* **Academic Term Length:** Two 14-week semesters (654 instructional contact hours) + One 14-week Co-op Work Term (420-560 hours)
* **Campus:** Ottawa (Woodroffe) Campus & Hybrid Delivery

---

## 1. Program Description

The **Business Intelligence Systems Infrastructure (BISI) - Big Data & Public Sector Specialization** delivers advanced technical training designed to bridge graduates with computational, engineering, or quantitative degrees into senior analytics engineering, big data architecture, and public service data roles.

Students engage in rigorous, hands-on architectural and programming challenges spanning multi-node distributed processing (PySpark, Delta Lake), enterprise transactional and analytical SQL optimization, event-driven streaming (Apache Kafka), and sovereign cloud deployment patterns (AWS and Azure Canada Central zones under Canadian Centre for Cyber Security ITSG-33 guidelines).

Uniquely positioned in Canada's National Capital Region, the program embeds compliance standards issued by the **Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat (TBS)**, including:
1. The **Policy on Service and Digital** and **Directive on Automated Decision-Making (Algorithmic Impact Assessments)**.
2. The **Standard on Web Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 Level AA)** applied to executive dashboards.
3. The **Official Languages Act**, requiring programmatic dynamic English/French localized data models.
4. **Protected B, Medium Integrity, Medium Availability (PBMM)** data protection protocols.

## 2. Admission Requirements

### 2.1 Academic Requirements
Applicants must hold an Ontario College Advanced Diploma (3-year), a Bachelor's Degree, or international equivalent from an accredited post-secondary institution in one of the following disciplines:
* Computer Science / Computing Science
* Software Engineering / Computer Systems Engineering
* Mathematics / Applied Statistics
* Information Technology / Database Administration
* Quantitative Economics or Business Information Systems with substantial programming coursework.

### 2.2 Prerequisite Subject Matter Competencies
* **Programming:** Minimum one post-secondary course in high-level programming (Python, Java, C++, or C#) with at least 65% (C+).
* **Databases:** Minimum one post-secondary course in Relational Databases and SQL with at least 65% (C+).
* **Mathematics:** Demonstrated coursework in Linear Algebra, Differential Calculus, or Inferential Statistics.

### 2.3 English Language Proficiency Requirements
Applicants whose native language is not English must satisfy one of the following benchmarks:
* **IELTS Academic:** Overall band score of 6.5 with no single band score below 6.0.
* **TOEFL iBT:** Overall score of 88 with minimums of 22 in Reading, 22 in Listening, 22 in Speaking, and 22 in Writing.
* **Duolingo English Test (DET):** Overall score of 120 with minimum 120 in Literacy and no sub-score below 105.

## 3. Vocational Learning Outcomes (VLOs)

In accordance with the Ontario Qualifications Framework (OQF Level 8), the graduate has reliably demonstrated the ability to:

1. **VLO 1 - Distributed Big Data Infrastructure:** Collect, ingest, transform, and partition massive semi-structured and unstructured datasets using distributed compute clusters (Apache Spark, PySpark, Delta Lake, Parquet).
2. **VLO 2 - Secure Database Architecture:** Architect, index, optimize, and administer relational and analytical database management systems under strict ACID transaction isolation, Row-Level Security (RLS), and RBAC controls.
3. **VLO 3 - Sovereign Public Sector Cloud:** Provision and harden cloud storage, identity federation, private networking, and compute infrastructure in Canadian sovereign cloud regions complying with CCCS ITSG-33 and PBMM guardrails.
4. **VLO 4 - Modern Data Pipeline Engineering:** Design and deploy automated, tested, containerized ELT/ETL pipelines utilizing dbt, Apache Airflow, and Git-driven CI/CD quality gates.
5. **VLO 5 - Accessible & Bilingual BI Storytelling:** Build high-performance analytical data models and visual dashboards (Power BI / Tableau) meeting WCAG 2.1 Level AA screen-reader standards and dynamic Official Languages (EN/FR) localization.
6. **VLO 6 - Applied Machine Learning & Policy Analytics:** Formulate, train, validate, and interpret predictive machine learning models for public policy decision support while systematically quantifying demographic parity and disparate impact.
7. **VLO 7 - Real-Time Streaming Systems:** Construct resilient streaming architectures using Apache Kafka and Spark Structured Streaming with watermarking, sliding windows, and exactly-once transactional sinks.
8. **VLO 8 - Enterprise Data Fabrics & Data Mesh:** Model complex enterprise data domains utilizing Data Vault 2.0 (Hubs, Links, Satellites), Master Data Management (MDM) entity resolution, and federated computational governance.
9. **VLO 9 - Algorithmic Governance & Responsible AI:** Conduct formal Treasury Board Algorithmic Impact Assessments (AIA) and deploy audited, private Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines with automated PII masking.
10. **VLO 10 - Collaborative Capstone Execution:** Lead and participate in multidisciplinary Agile/Scrum teams to solve an authentic public sector or industry client data challenge from inception through formal executive defense.

## 4. Graduation & Credential Requirements

To be awarded the **Ontario College Graduate Certificate in Business Intelligence Systems Infrastructure**:
1. Students must achieve a passing grade of at least 50% (D-) in every academic course.
2. Students must maintain a cumulative Grade Point Average (GPA) of at least 2.0 (C average) across all Level 01 and Level 02 courses.
3. Students enrolled in the Co-op version must successfully complete `WKT-8001` including employer evaluation and a satisfactory Work Term Report.
