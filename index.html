Program Structure & Vocational Outcomes

Modeled directly after Algonquin College's Business Intelligence System Infrastructure (BISI) (Program Code 1514X03FWO), this curriculum incorporates distributed big data frameworks (PySpark, Delta Lake, Snowflake, Kafka) with the governance mandates of the Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat (TBS) and the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (CCCS).

⚡ Term 1: Fall Semester

Distributed computing fundamentals, enterprise SQL tuning, PBMM cloud landing zones, machine learning policy analytics, and dbt/Airflow lakehouse automation.

7 Courses | 336 Hours Level 01

🚀 Term 2: Winter Semester

Real-time Kafka streaming, Databricks Unity Catalog, Data Vault 2.0 modeling, TBS Algorithmic Impact Assessments, and the Client-Sponsored Capstone Project.

6 Courses | 318 Hours Level 02

💼 Term 3: Summer Co-op

14-week competitive paid placement within federal departments (StatsCan, CRA, DND, SSC), Crown corporations (Bank of Canada), or NCR tech enterprises.

420–560 Hours Co-op 01

BISI-101: Big Data Programming & Distributed Computing with PySpark

56 Hours ▼

Core Textbooks: Martin Kleppmann (2017) Designing Data-Intensive Applications; Matei Zaharia et al. (2010) Spark: Cluster Computing with Working Sets.

Module 01: Distributed Runtime Internals & Memory Layouts
Examines RDD lineage graphs, DAGScheduler stage boundaries, shuffle spill metrics, and Tungsten off-heap binary memory management.
Module 02: Advanced Join Mechanics & Key Salting
Deconstructs Broadcast Hash Joins vs. Sort-Merge Joins. Teaches key salting formulas to remediate 90%+ data skew in public procurement feeds.
Module 03: Delta Lake ACID Transactions & Time Travel
Deep dive into the _delta_log commit protocol, optimistic concurrency control, and zero-downtime file compaction.

Federal Security Clearance & Co-op Bridging Guide

A structured roadmap guiding students from Day 1 through to Reliability/Secret clearance and permanent public service appointment.

Month 1–2 (Sept–Oct): Security Clearance Dossier Assembly
Students in GEP-1001 assemble 5-year contiguous address history, employment contacts, digital fingerprinting receipts with Commissionaires, and submit Form TBS/SCT 330-23E.
Month 4–5 (Dec–Jan): Reliability Status & Co-op Matching
Reliability Status is granted by Departmental Security Officers. Students compete on HireAC and the Federal Student Work Experience Program (FSWEP) for paid positions.
Month 9–12 (May–Aug): 14-Week Paid Co-op Work Term
Students work full-time on production data pipelines (IT-01/IT-02), receiving faculty site visits and submitting formal technical reports.
Graduation: Student Bridging to Indeterminate Positions
Under the Public Service Employment Act, managers bridge successful co-op graduates directly into permanent indeterminate positions without public competition!
