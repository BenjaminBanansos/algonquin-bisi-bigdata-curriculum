# Government of Canada Student Bridging & Employment Playbook

* **Target Classifications:** IT-01 / IT-02 (Computer Systems / Data Solutions), EC-02 / EC-04 (Economics and Social Sciences)
* **Authority:** Public Service Employment Act (PSEA) Student Bridging Mechanism

---

## 1. What is Student Bridging?
Student Bridging allows federal hiring managers to appoint graduating students who previously completed a student employment program (Co-op, FSWEP, RAP) to permanent or term indeterminate positions **without conducting a public competition**.

---

## 2. Eligibility Requirements for Bridging
1. Must have successfully completed at least one 14-week recognized Co-op work term (`WKT-8001`).
2. Must have graduated with the Ontario College Graduate Certificate credential.
3. Must satisfy the Statement of Merit Criteria (SoMC) for the target group and level.
4. Must maintain an active Canadian Government Security Clearance (Reliability Status minimum).

---

## 3. High-Demand Public Sector Data Classifications

### IT Group (Information Technology)
* **IT-01 (Junior Data Engineer / BI Developer):** Focuses on ETL pipelines, SQL queries, database administration, and report building.
* **IT-02 (Senior Technical Analyst / Lakehouse Specialist):** Focuses on PySpark, cloud infrastructure, streaming Kafka, and enterprise architecture.

### EC Group (Economics and Social Science Services)
* **EC-02 / EC-04 (Quantitative Data Analyst / Modeler):** Focuses on statistical modeling, Scikit-Learn policy analytics, data visualization, and briefings for Deputy Ministers.
