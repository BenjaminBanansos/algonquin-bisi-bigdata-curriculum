# GEP-2001: Co-Op Competition, Federal Pool Match & Technical Interviews

* **Course Code:** GEP-2001
* **Contact Hours:** 10.0 Hours (Field Coaching & Seminar)
* **Delivery Mode:** Career Advisory / Placement Support
* **Prerequisites:** GEP-1001

---

## 1. Course Description
Active placement phase. Students engage directly in competitive job matching across HireAC, federal student employment pools (FSWEP, Post-Secondary Co-op), and private sector recruitment. Covers interview prep for Government of Canada competency evaluations (IT-01/IT-02 and EC-02/EC-04 classifications), live technical challenges, offer negotiations, and bridging paths into permanent public service appointments upon graduation.
