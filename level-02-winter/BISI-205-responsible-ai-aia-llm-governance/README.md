# BISI-205: ML 2: TBS Algorithmic Impact Assessment, Responsible AI & LLMs

* **Course Code:** BISI-205
* **Contact Hours:** 56.0 Hours (4.0 hrs/week × 14 weeks)
* **Delivery Mode:** Applied AI & Governance Studio
* **Prerequisites:** BISI-101, BISI-104

---

## 1. Course Description
Examines advanced applied artificial intelligence through the rigorous regulatory framework of the Government of Canada. Analyzes the **Treasury Board Secretariat (TBS) Directive on Automated Decision-Making** and mandates for Algorithmic Impact Assessments (AIA Levels I through IV). Students build private, sovereign **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** systems over federal policy documents using vector databases (ChromaDB), LangChain, and open LLMs while integrating automated PII sanitization (Microsoft Presidio) to guarantee privacy and ATIP compliance.
